Find a group

A house of prayer, and a home for the people in it.

We are a community of believers who gather to worship, learn the Scriptures and serve our neighbours. Some of us have been here for decades, and some walked in for the first time last month. Everyone is welcome at the same table.

Our doors are open to families, students, workers and anyone searching. You will find honest teaching, joyful singing and people who will learn your name.

I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord. Psalm 122:1

Find your place to serve and grow

Every ministry meets on a regular schedule and welcomes new members.

Children

Bible stories, songs and games for ages 3 to 12 during both Sunday services.

Youth

A space for teenagers and young adults to ask real questions and build lasting friendships.

Women

Monthly fellowship, mentoring and prayer for women of every season of life.

Men

Saturday morning breakfast, Scripture study and service projects around the community.

Choir and music

Rehearsals every Thursday evening. Singers and instrumentalists are always needed.

Outreach

Food drives, school support and visits to the sick, led by volunteers each month.
